# Stock logo assets

These 28 local SVGs identify the companies in `src/game/catalog.ts`. The icons load from the game itself, so a slow or blocked third-party favicon service cannot remove the brand marks.

- `RELIANCE.svg`: [Simple Icons, Reliance Industries Limited](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/relianceindustrieslimited.svg), downloaded from package version 16.35.0. Simple Icons distributes its icon collection under CC0; the company mark remains a trademark of its owner.
- The other 27 SVGs: [Indian Listed Company Logos](https://github.com/dharunashokkumar/indian-listed-company-logos/tree/main/data/logos/nse), from its NSE assets. That collection attributes the artwork to TradingView's company logo feed and notes that logos remain trademarks of their owners. Its repository license applies to its code, while company logos remain third-party marks.

`TATAMOTORS.svg` comes from the collection's `NSE_TMPV.svg`, the current Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles listing after the company split. `TCS.svg`, `TATAMOTORS.svg`, `TATASTEEL.svg`, and `TATACONSUM.svg` use the Tata group mark supplied for those listings. Their filenames still correspond to the game's symbols. A generic sector glyph appears only if a local SVG cannot load.
